One scope concern in view_protect: PAGE_GUARD is not a caching modifier. [Microsoft documents it as a one-time access alarm](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/memory/memory-protection-constants). Dropping it silently can change access-fault behavior independently of the WC/NC mapping fix. Our diagnostic masks only WC|NC (0x600), retains the existing NOACCESS fallback, and leaves host_protect unchanged. Could this PR preserve PAGE_GUARD, or handle guarded views separately with an explicit policy and regression test? The added test currently covers WC, not guard semantics or NOCACHE.
